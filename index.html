PROJECT21 is a brand strategy and creative studio. We build complete brand programs for new tea brands and for retail brands going overseas — finding the cultural thread inside each category, and turning business strategy into identities that read internationally.

Strategy, made visible. Value, made to last.

PROJECT21 是一家品牌創意工作室，專注新茶飲與出海零售的全案設計。我們在品類之中找出文化切口，把商業策略轉化為具國際審美的視覺語言，幫助品牌在海外市場直接面對消費者。

讓策略被看見，讓價值被記住。
